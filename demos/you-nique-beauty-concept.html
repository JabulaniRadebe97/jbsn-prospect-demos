The idea

Make the first impression feel as thoughtful as the visit.

This concept imagines a simple, elegant customer journey: discover the service, ask a question, then request an appointment. It is designed around the public social presence for You-Nique Beauty and is intended for discussion—not as a claim that the business has approved this design.

Rather than inventing a price list or availability, the proposed experience invites customers to confirm the treatment and preferred time directly with the business.

Service discovery

A clearer path from interest to enquiry.

Based on publicly visible posts, this concept groups the services into simple starting points. Final service names, details and pricing would be confirmed by the business before any production launch.

01 /

Nails & finishing touches

Help clients start an enquiry about a manicure or nail look, with space to describe the style they have in mind.

02 /

Waxing & smooth-skin care

A discreet service entry point where visitors can ask about the treatment and suitable appointment times.

03 /

Beauty appointments

A simple enquiry route for other currently offered treatments, confirmed directly with the business.

The next step

Let’s find a time that works for you.

A proposed appointment enquiry flow could collect the service of interest, preferred day and a short note—then open a message for the customer to review and send themselves.

Brand identity direction

Intimate, refined, self-expressive

A botanical monogram direction pairs the concept’s plum, rose and warm neutral palette with restrained movement. It is a proposed mark only; final beauty branding and services require owner approval.

Logo: vector-first concept lockup for crisp display at every size. Colour: palette sampled from the existing site direction or public-facing cues and refined for contrast. Motion: a signature mark animation, layered image reveal and purposeful hover/scroll transitions—not generic decoration.
